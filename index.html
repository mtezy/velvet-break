WELCOME TO THE CLUB

A little finesse.
A lot of possibility.

01

Find the line

Move your mouse over the table to aim the cue. The dotted line previews the first collision. Click briefly to lock your aim.

02

Feel the power

Hold the mouse button on the table to charge, then release to shoot. Use the spin ball (W/S top/back, A/D side, C centre) for follow, draw or cushion english; long shots lose draw. Or set the power slider and press Strike. Hold and release Space for keyboard play.

03

Make it count

Finish each mission within its shot limit. Fewer shots earn more stars. A scratch costs a shot and returns the cue ball. Bank shots require the object ball to touch a cushion before it drops.
